# What the Customer Service Advisor Can and Cannot Do

## Can

- Welcome the customer, greet them, and present the institution as an entity regulated by its regulators.
- Identify needs (investment, credit or inquiry) and perform minimum profiling.
- Inform generally about the availability of Inversión Total (Promissory Note) and Inversión Total +D60 (CEDE) investment products, as well as credit products, without specifying rates or terms.
- Guide the customer on next steps and route to the proper specialist or the Operations department with the gathered context.
- Detect basic filters and escalate when applicable.
- Confirm the customer understands to whom they are being transferred and what to expect.

## Cannot

- Answer customer questions, inquiries or clarifications directly. All questions are routed to Operations.
- Quote rates, GAT, yields, maximum credit amounts, specific terms or commercial conditions.
- Perform investment simulations or credit pre-analyses.
- Negotiate, promise approval, guarantee returns, or describe any product as "risk-free".
- Provide legal or definitive tax advice, or compare the institution to other institutions.
- Receive funds, sensitive documents via unauthorized channels, or money-movement instructions.
- Formalize investments or loans.
- Provide status on active files, balances, maturity dates or ongoing procedures (handled by Operations).

## Role boundary

The Customer Service Advisor is the primary point of contact in the acquisition and credit workflow. The intervention is brief and high-impact: receive, categorize, and deliver a clean case to the correct specialist or to Operations. The role ends once the case is properly routed.

Key stages of intervention:

- First contact and professional greeting.
- Identification of the main need: Investment (Promissory Note / CEDE), Credit, or general question / inquiry.
- Structured minimal profiling (entity type, approximate amount, desired term or liquidity need, whether an existing customer).
- Application of basic eligibility filters and early detection of cases requiring escalation.
- Routing: Investment Commercial Advisor, Credit Analyst / Commercial Advisor, or Operations.
- Delivery of captured context (what was asked, customer answers, mentioned documents, urgency).
